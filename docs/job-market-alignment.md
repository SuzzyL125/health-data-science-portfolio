# Job-market alignment

Reviewed August 2026.

## Best-fit role families

### 1. Healthcare or Population Health Data Scientist

Strongest overall fit. These roles commonly combine claims, EHR, or registry data with Python/R/SQL, model evaluation, causal inference, survival analysis, reproducible pipelines, and communication with clinical teams.

Portfolio evidence: the Parkinson's classification case study, clinical interpretation, model comparison, and repeated-measures discussion.

### 2. Real-World Evidence Data Scientist

Strong fit because of epidemiology training, SEER-Medicare research, longitudinal design, causal inference, CDISC experience, and regulated deliverables. Predictive ML is useful here, but rigorous study design and fit-for-purpose data assessment are often more important than deep learning.

Portfolio evidence: transparent method selection, limitations, validation thinking, and statistical-computing workflow.

### 3. Clinical Data Scientist

Strong fit for roles using EHR, claims, registry, or clinical-trial data. The differentiator is the combination of clinical-data literacy, data dictionaries, statistical analysis, reporting, and stakeholder consultation.

Portfolio evidence: clinically framed ML project and computing foundations; broader resume evidence should feature Epic, REDCap, SDTM/ADaM, and regulatory TFLs.

### 4. Epidemiologist-Data Scientist

Excellent bridge role between formal epidemiologic design and modern analytics. These positions value R/Python/SQL, visualization, machine learning, causal inference, and real-world data experience.

## Current gaps to address honestly

- Add a fully reproducible Python healthcare ML project with a public dataset, tests, and environment lockfile.
- Show more production SQL: joins, window functions, data-quality checks, and cohort construction.
- Demonstrate Git/GitHub workflow through clean commits, issues, and project documentation.
- Add one interactive deliverable in Shiny, Streamlit, or Quarto.
- Add cloud or distributed-computing evidence only after hands-on work; do not imply AWS, Azure, GCP, or Spark production experience from coursework alone.
- Strengthen ML evaluation with patient-level data splitting, calibration, class-imbalance handling, and external validation.

## Representative market signals

- Population-health postings emphasize claims/EHR/registry data, Python and SQL, survival models, gradient boosting, causal inference, clinical utility, reproducible pipelines, and communication with clinical operations.
- Real-world evidence postings emphasize epidemiology, fit-for-purpose data assessment, data quality, regulatory-aligned evidence generation, and R/Python/SQL.
- Epidemiologist-data-scientist postings seek population-level estimates from surveillance, claims, and clinical data alongside statistical modeling, machine learning, and visualization.

## Recommended positioning statement

> Epidemiologist and health data scientist who combines causal inference and longitudinal study design with predictive modeling, reproducible analytics, and hands-on experience with claims, EHR, REDCap, and regulated clinical data.
